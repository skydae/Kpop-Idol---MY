# 🎤 K-GEMPAK ENT: K-Pop Agency & Survival Simulator **Game Design Blueprint & Single-File Playable Web Prototype** --- ## 1. Core Gameplay Loop Overview Inspired by *Monthly Entertainment (608 Factory)* and real-world YG Entertainment trainee systems (*WIN*, *YG Treasure Box*, *Last Evaluation*), this web-first simulator runs on a **12-Month Yearly Cycle**: 1. **Months 1–12 (Daily/Monthly Agency Grind):** * **Global Auditions:** Scout trainees from **Kuala Lumpur (Bukit Bintang)**, **Seoul (Hapjeong)**, **Bangkok**, and **Tokyo**. * **YG-Style Brutal Training:** Trainees grind **Vocal**, **Rap**, **Dance**, and **YG Swag (Star Power)** while managing **Stamina** and **Ego/Dorm Drama**. * **Monthly Evaluations:** Every month, trainees face the **English Pop + 16-Bar Self-Written Rap Check**. Ranks shift from **Rank F to Rank SS+**, and Team A seats are constantly at risk. 2. **Normal Path — In-House Permanent Group Debut & Comebacks:** * Select 3–5 top trainees to form a permanent agency group. * Produce albums, choose concepts (*Hip-Hop Street, Girl/Boy Crush, Cyber Y2K, Melodic Ballad*), and promote on **4 Weekly Music Shows** (*The Stage, M-Beatdown, K-Bank, Inki-Pop*). * **Live Uncut Encore Vocal Check:** Winning #1 triggers an immediate unedited vocal check. High vocals go viral for "Eating the CD"; low vocals crack on stage and get roasted on K-Threads! 3. **Month 8 Annual Sub-Event — Multi-Agency Survival Show:** * Every year in Month 8, a randomly named broadcast survival show launches (e.g., *PRODUCE STARLIGHT 999*, *PLANET ODYSSEY X*). * Send 1–3 trainees to battle 50+ trainees from rival Big 4 agencies in **1v1 Position Swap Battles**, survive **Mnet-style Evil Editing**, and fight for the **Top 7 Project Group** or poach eliminated SS-rank stars! 4. **Month 12 Climax — Year-End Annual Award Shows (MAMA / MMA):** * Direct your group's **7-Minute Special Rearrangement Stage** (Rock Remix, Traditional Malaysian-Korean Fusion, Hand-Mic Cypher, or Boycott Diss Track). * Compete for **Rookie of the Year (ROTY)**, **Bonsang**, and the **4 Grand Daesangs**, then export your **Threads Shareable Flex Card**! --- ## 2.
